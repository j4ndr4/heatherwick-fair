A tale of Heatherwick

The Fair


A letter arrives. Only some intriguing lines. You will understand that some demands were never meant to be met by ordinary means.

In the quiet coastal village of Heatherwick, young Elspie has no money and no clear path. Her mother can only tell her the truth: the message is an old riddle, and she must make her own way.

So begins The Fair — a gentle, atmospheric role-playing game of impossible tasks, quiet magic, and the slow unravelling of an ancient love.

Wander the strands and the forest. Speak with villagers. Trade what the land gives you.

Inspired by the traditional ballad Scarborough Fair and its older, stranger root in The Elfin Knight, the game invites you into a world of hidden territories, leather sickles, and proofs of love that can only be answered with wit, patience, and the quiet courage of the North Country.
